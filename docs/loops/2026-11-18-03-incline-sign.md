## The questions as answered

**What should change.** The TV speech at BLK 8 (`320,580 73x135`) is drawn
leaning the wrong way: the report's crop shows it about 20° counter-clockwise
(bottom-right), while the source columns lean about 5° clockwise (bottom-left).
The label has to follow the ink, and the reader's `lean` must never decide it.

**How to verify.** `npm run regression -- incline` now:

- selects the labels by the group whose source matches `やつ|やっ`, not by a
  rectangle that also catches other text;
- asserts the label's lean is 5° ±4°;
- asserts the font is 22px ±30% for a label of at most 8 glyphs (a reader run
  that merges all columns into one line is a layout failure, not a glyph
  measurement);
- runs `inclineSelfTest`, which constructs a region with a reader lean of −20°
  and no mask and fails if the label is not left upright.

The rendered page is `out/regression/incline.png`; the guards are the other nine
cases, typecheck, and the rebuilt `dist`.

**Done when.** The label at BLK 8 leans +5° ±4°, a reader-only lean cannot rotate

## The check, and the baseline

The reported artifact is the before image:
[`assets/incline-sign-before.jpg`](assets/incline-sign-before.jpg) — the text
runs top-right to bottom-left of the *label*, about −20°.

The old fallback was one line:

```ts
const groupLean = measured?.lean ?? (reported[0] ?? 0);
```

`measured` is the ink's major axis. `reported` is the reader's `lean`, which on
this page came back `0` for a 5° column and `−7` for a 15.5° one, sign reversed.
When the mask was not in hand — a stale build, or a call that did not pass it —
the reader's guess became the label's rotation. That is the −20° in the report.

The probe at 03:29 on the current source, with the mask, measured `+0.0957 rad`
= `+5.48°` for all three regions of BLK 8. So the ink path was already right;
the fallback was the bug.

## What was left over

- **The TV speech can still be one line.** When the reader returns all three
  columns as one entry, the translation is squeezed into one line at 15px. The
  angle is right and the font rule skips it, but the picture is worse than the
  2–3 column runs. Splitting one entry back into its columns is the merge/layout
  change noted in the previous loop.
- **The banner is not reported every run.** The `31` and the vertical title
  sometimes come back as two entries, sometimes not at all; the rule skips the
  banner when it is absent rather than failing on the reader's luck.
- **A mask-less browser build draws upright labels.** That is deliberate: the
  ink is the only source of a sign. If the seg plane is genuinely unavailable,
  the next step is a page-pixel skew estimate, not the reader's lean.
